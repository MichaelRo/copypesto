# 0058. What the Mac's AI, Ask, Decode JWT and smart collections look like on iPhone and iPad

Status: Accepted

Applies ADR 0003 (secrets are masked and shown on request), ADR 0005 (no network but a short list, and AI on the device) and ADR 0020 (Decode JWT) to the iOS app of ADR 0046. Nothing they decide is reversed.

## Context
Pass 3 of the iOS app brings over the Mac features that make sense on a phone and a tablet: Decode JWT, Copy As JSON and Markdown, a GIF that stays whole through the share sheet, AI actions and Describe Image, Ask Pesto and the smart-collection builder. The Mac shows its answers in a floating panel and its filters in a bar; neither exists on iOS.

## Decision
- **Decode JWT** is in the menu of a hidden JWT clip under the same gate as Show: it asks through `SecretReveal` and the shared `AuthenticationGrace` window, and is offered only when `CardConcealment.allowsDecode` and the device can authenticate. The header and payload open in a sheet that holds them in `MobileAppState.decodedJWT` only: the lock, a clear and leaving the foreground drop it, it is never stored, searched, logged or sent to the model. Copy Payload writes through `MobileClipboard` as a secret (this device only, gone in two minutes) and is the only way out: the decoded text is not selectable (on the Mac popover neither), and the sheet closes when the content cover goes up (a screen capture with the setting on, Hide Clip Contents), at the lock and on leaving the foreground. An answer that arrives after the lock or while Pesto is away is dropped.
- **Copy As JSON and Markdown** use `StructuredPaste`, unchanged and already portable; they lead the Copy As menu of a clip that qualifies and the Mac's refusals (hidden, filtered, pending, too big, locked) apply.
- **A GIF stays whole.** `SharedContentChoice` picks a GIF over the still copies an app registers beside it; a GIF over the inbox limit is refused, not reduced to one frame; sharing a GIF out hands the system a `.gif` file (`ShareFile`, swept after an hour) and `ShareLink` exports it as a GIF. That file is plaintext in the temporary folder, so it is written with complete file protection, removed when the share sheet finishes, and the whole `pesto-share` folder is removed at launch, at the History Lock and on Clear History (iOS has no other wipe). It is kept while Pesto is in the background, because some share targets send Pesto there mid-share; the end of the share, the lock, Clear History or the next launch removes it.
- **AI actions are a submenu, the answer a sheet.** `AIActionsMenu` offers the Mac's actions for the clip's kind through `CardAIActions`, which follows `AIAvailability.presentation`: hidden when the device can never run the model, greyed out with `AIAvailability.message` when the person can fix it or turned it off. A secret, a covered, filtered or pending clip and a locked history are offered nothing, so the model never sees a mask or a secret. The sheet shows the answer, says when the model read only the start of a clip (`AIResult.Coverage.note`) and has Copy, which keeps the answer as a clip as Copy Recognized Text does (ADR 0037); it is gone with Done and the lock. The answer is dropped with Done, the lock and leaving the foreground. A long Summarize or Key Points is **not** read a part at a time on iOS: the model reads what its budget allows and the answer says how much (the Mac's chunked reading, `AIRun`, stays Mac-only until a sheet for it is designed). One switch (Settings → Apple Intelligence, `Setting.appleIntelligenceEnabled`) covers every AI feature, as on the Mac.
- **Ask Pesto is a chip, not a sheet.** While the search field holds words an **Ask** chip leads the filters; it turns the sentence into words, kinds and a date range with Core's `FallbackQueryInterpreter` (Apple's model when it can run, `RuleBasedQueryInterpreter` otherwise or on failure, so it works with Apple Intelligence off). The words go to the field, the kinds and range to the filter, and the chip becomes one chip naming them (Links · today) whose tap takes the filters off and keeps the words. A kind or date chip chosen afterwards takes over its half. An answer applies only to the search it was asked about (not after the words changed, the search ended or the history locked), and the lock clears its filters. A sheet from the toolbar would hide the grid the filters act on and add a fourth control to a bar that has three.
- **Smart collections are built in the collection editor.** New Collection has a Plain/Smart switch; Smart adds the rules as Form sections (groups of rules, all/any at both levels, as the Mac's builder), each control offering only what `PredicateValidator` allows, so a rule is valid as made; a text rule must have words before Save. A bundle id is offered only when a rule already uses it. A collection made by a newer Pesto, whose rules this version cannot read, keeps them when its name, icon or color is edited. Saving is one change for Undo (`CollectionStore`). The kind of a collection is chosen at creation only. Rows are found by id, never by position, so removing a rule cannot hand its neighbour the change; saving rewrites the stored rules only when they changed. On both platforms the boolean device rule reads "Copied on" + "another device" / "this device" ("this Mac").

## Consequences
- The iOS app offers on-device AI where the Mac does and nowhere else; a device without Apple Intelligence sees none of it (or sees it greyed out with the reason).
- A long text summarized on iOS is summarized from its start, and says so.
- Tested: `CardCopyActionsTests`, `JWTDecodingTextTests`, `GIFSharingTests`, `GIFSharingInTests`, `SharedInboxTests` (choice and size handling), `CardAIActionsTests`, `MobileAITests`, `AskPestoLibraryTests`, `SmartCollectionEditingTests`, `Pass3UITests`.
