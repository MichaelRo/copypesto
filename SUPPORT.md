# Getting help

- **How do I…?** Start with the [user guide](docs/guide/README.md) and the [FAQ](docs/guide/faq.md).
- **Something is broken.** Check [Troubleshooting](docs/guide/troubleshooting.md), then open a
  [bug report](https://github.com/MichaelRo/copypesto/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). Include your Pesto and macOS
  versions.
- **An idea or question.** Start a discussion in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/MichaelRo/copypesto/discussions) or open a [feature request](https://github.com/MichaelRo/copypesto/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **A security or privacy problem.** Do not post it publicly. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Anything else.** hello@copypesto.net

Pesto is free and maintained by one person, so replies can take a while.
