# Undo and redo: what they cost (2026-10-18, ADR 0008)

Undo keeps sealed snapshots in memory and records an entry where a person's action is made. Nothing runs on ingest,
search, scroll or opening the shelf, so those budgets are unchanged by design; this note says how that is checked and
what the recording and restoring cost.

## Run it

`make bench-undo` builds the hosted tests and runs `UndoPerformanceTests` and `UndoHistoryMemoryTests`, printing the
`PERF` lines (the full log is `.build/xcode/bench-undo.log`). Budgets are asserted on a developer Mac only
(`RunEnvironment.isCI`); on CI the numbers are measured and the structural assertions (what was held, what came back)
still run. Run `make bench-scroll` and `scripts/check-memory-budget.sh` before and after a change that touches the
shelf, and compare with the baseline below.

## Budgets

| What | Measured by | Budget |
|---|---|---|
| Recording a delete of 1, 50 or 200 clips, over the same delete without it | `UndoPerformanceTests.test_whenDeletingClips_...` | adds under 100 ms |
| Undo of those deletes (a re-insert with the original ids, one save) | same | under 100 ms |
| One recorded edit, over an unrecorded one | `test_whenEditingClips_thenRecordingCostsLittle` | adds under 5 ms |
| A drag that reorders 200 clips in a collection (one action) | same | under 100 ms |
| A full stack (20 entries) slows the copy path | `test_whenTheStackIsFull_thenIngestIsNoSlower` | median within 1.5x plus 5 ms |
| The stack at its limits holds no more than its budget | `UndoHistoryMemoryTests` | 20 entries and 16 MB held; footprint grows less than 16 MB plus 24 MB |

## Baseline before Undo (measured by the first session on a developer Mac)

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Memory, shelf open / closed | 83 / 55 MB (budget 150 MB) |
| Overlay open to first paint | 41 ms (budget 100 ms) |

## Results

Measured on Apple Silicon via `make bench-undo`, `scripts/check-memory-budget.sh` and `OverlayPerformanceTests`:

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| `undo delete 1 / 50 / 200 clips` | 1 clip: +0.0 ms rec, 1.6 ms undo; 50 clips: +2.4 ms rec, 14.1 ms undo; 200 clips: +9.6 ms rec, 39.6 ms undo |
| `undo edit one clip`, `undo reorder 200 clips` | edit: 0.43 ms (vs 0.41 ms plain); reorder 200: 29.61 ms, held 154 KB |
| `undo ingest 2 KB text`, empty and full stack | empty stack: 2.35 ms; full stack: 2.26 ms |
| `undo memory` | 18 entries holding 15.2 MB of a 16 MB budget; footprint grew 6.1 MB |
| Memory, shelf open / closed, with a full stack | 52 / 30 MB (budget 150 MB) |
| Overlay open to first paint | ~40 ms (budget 100 ms) |
